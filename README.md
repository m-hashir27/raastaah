# Raasta — Karachi Public Transport Planner

**Making Karachi's public transport easier to navigate.**

Raasta is a frontend prototype exploring how route planning could be integrated into the existing public transport information experience in Karachi. It helps users explore available routes, compare journey options, and view estimated travel times.

## Features

- **Route planning:** Enter your starting point and destination to explore possible journeys.
- **Journey recommendations:** Highlights a suggested journey from the available route data.
- **Transfer suggestions:** Shows possible journeys involving a change of bus or service.
- **Estimated travel times:** Provides approximate journey durations based on stop-to-stop estimates.
- **Responsive interface:** Designed for desktop and mobile screens.
- **Multilingual safety notices:** Includes English, Roman Urdu, and Urdu reminders to confirm routes before boarding.

## Important Limitations

Raasta is an independent prototype, not an official SMTA product.

- Route and stop information is manually compiled and may be incomplete or inaccurate.
- Journey recommendations and transfer suggestions depend on the available prototype data.
- Travel times are estimates, not live predictions or official schedules.
- Live vehicle tracking, verified fares, and real-time service updates are not currently implemented.

Always confirm a bus's route with its conductor before boarding. For Green Line, confirm at the station counter.
